How it works

  • Table columns: ID, Task, Level (0 = top, 1 = inside the summary above…), Duration (hrs), Predecessors, Fixed Start, % Complete. Start and Finish are filled in for you.
  • Summary rows are rows followed by deeper-level rows; their dates and hours roll up.
  • Predecessors: IDs separated by commas. 5 = after 5 finishes, 5SS = start with 5, 5FF = finish with 5, 5+2h = 2 hours after, 5-1d = 1 working day of overlap.
  • Drag a bar to move it (sets Fixed Start). Drag its right end to change the hours. Drag the dot after a bar onto another bar to link them.
  • Tap a task to see and edit it; the Excel row is selected too. Edits in Excel update the chart.
  • Ctrl + scroll zooms. Drag the edge of the task list to resize it.
